Three destination customs for a TIR truck: which one to name

A quote is written against a destination customs, not a city. We quote three destination customs, and the cargo is delivered to the yard of the one you name.

Three destination customs for a TIR truck
Destination customsDelivery pointWhat it requires
Tehran West CustomsTehran West Customs yardImport clearance filed in the Tehran customs district
Aprin Dry PortAprin dry port yardUnloading and clearance inside the Aprin terminal
Mashhad CustomsMashhad Customs yardImport clearance filed in the Mashhad customs district

Sarakhs and Lotfabad are entry crossings into Iran, not delivery points. Delivery is to the destination customs yard on CPT / DAP terms; import clearance and duties sit with the buyer and the buyer's licensed broker.

Guides to reading transit times

Table times are counted from the Wuqia border; these guides help you plan the load.

Why TIR does not cut days

Choosing TIR does not change the transit time table.

What a truck transfer on the way means

A direct service transfers once in Bukhara; cargo is opened less, not never.

Cargo from several suppliers in one trip

One trailer, several separately received cargoes; the volume and weight caps apply to the total.

Where a TIR truck delivery ends

Customs yard, not the buyer's door; Sarakhs and Lotfabad are entry crossings, not delivery points.

06

TIR truck route FAQ

Do the table times include the China domestic leg?

No. They start at the Wuqia border crossing; the China hub to Wuqia leg adds about 7 days separately and is not added into one committed total.

Does the Khorgos route have published transit times?

No. Transit time and transfer arrangements for Khorgos are confirmed case by case.

How often is the truck transloaded?

A direct truck on Wuqia transloads once, at Bukhara. Transshipment service transloads at Osh and Bukhara, and some shipments once more at the Iranian border.

Where does the truck enter Iran?

Entry is at Sarakhs, or Lotfabad for road freight. Clearance inside Iran is arranged by the buyer; we do not clear.

Which destination customs should I name in the enquiry?

One of three: Tehran West Customs, Aprin Dry Port or Mashhad Customs. Sarakhs and Lotfabad are entry crossings, not delivery points.
