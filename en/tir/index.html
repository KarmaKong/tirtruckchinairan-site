TIR trucks and cargo for quotation

The 13.6 m curtainsider semitrailer carries up to 80 CBM and 22 tonnes, with both limits applying to each truck. Full truckload and groupage services are available, with groupage starting at 1 CBM or 100 kg. Any portion above the truck limits is quoted separately.

Send an enquiry for:

  • Solar and power equipment: photovoltaic modules, industrial inverters and transformers.
  • Industrial machinery: injection-moulding machines, CNC equipment and industrial presses. Include dimensions to confirm loading arrangements.
  • Industrial polymers and chemical materials: polymer masterbatch and polyurethane materials. Include the MSDS with your enquiry.

Volume and weight bind at the same time. Light, bulky cargo reaches the 80 CBM ceiling first; dense cargo reaches 22 tonnes with the trailer still part-empty. Anything above either limit for a single truck is quoted separately instead of being loaded over the cap.

Guides on trailer type and cargo

If you are unsure between a full load and groupage, these four guides settle it.

How several suppliers fill one trailer

Counted separately, sealed once for the whole trailer; groupage starts at 1 CBM or 100 kg.

What TIR changes in practice

Seal, carnet and fewer transfers; transit time and freight stay the same.

TIR and freight: what does not change

Choosing TIR does not create another price; volume above one trailer is priced separately.

Where a TIR truck delivery ends

Customs yard, not the buyer's door; Sarakhs and Lotfabad are entry crossings, not delivery points.

04

TIR truck capacity and cargo FAQ

How much cargo fits on one TIR truck?

80 CBM and 22 tonnes per truck, and both limits apply at the same time. Whichever is reached first caps the load.

Is the trailer a container?

No. It is a 13.6 m curtainsider semitrailer loaded from the side, and the seal goes on at the China warehouse.

Can two or three suppliers share one truck?

Yes. On TIR, groupage starts at 1 CBM or 100 kg; each supplier's cargo is counted separately and the trailer is sealed once.

What happens if my cargo is above 80 CBM or 22 tonnes?

The excess is quoted separately, usually as a second truck. Cargo is not loaded over the cap on one trailer.

Which cargo needs documents before a quote?

Chemicals, dangerous goods and branded cargo are reviewed first and are not accepted automatically. Lithium batteries need a full 16-section MSDS and a UN packaging certificate.
