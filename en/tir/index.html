What the TIR carnet does on a China–Iran shipment

Carnet, customs seal, transit control and transloading: the documents and handoffs that matter to an importer or customs broker.

01

The carnet and the guarantee

The TIR carnet is an international customs document linked to the TIR guarantee system. It is not a commercial invoice, transport insurance or an import-clearance certificate.

02

Vouchers and counterfoils

Customs complete the relevant vouchers (volets) and counterfoils (souches) as each TIR operation proceeds. The holder, goods manifest, vehicle details and seal references must correspond to the actual movement.

03

Controls en route

Under the TIR Convention, intact customs seals are generally accepted by customs en route. Customs retain the right to examine goods in exceptional or suspected-irregularity cases.

04

Opening and resealing

An authorised examination and any new seals must be recorded in the relevant carnet entries. Accidents, broken seals or cargo damage require the prescribed customs reporting procedure. A transload is not permission to open or reseal cargo independently.

05

Confirm the handoff before departure

Ask where and how many times cargo is transloaded, which authorised holder and carnet cover each operation, how customs supervision and records are maintained, and which destination office receives the goods. No specific TIR/IRU authorisation is claimed for this brand.

06

Wuqia · Direct configuration

The Wuqia direct configuration still includes one transload in Bukhara.

Wuqia · Transload configuration

Wuqia → Osh: transload → Bukhara: transload → Iran. Depending on the agreed configuration, another transload may occur at the Iran border. “Direct” refers to the one-Bukhara-transload plan; it does not mean no transload.

07

Handover at the customs terminal

CPT / DAP, with the named place and scope specified in each quotation. Destinations: Shahriyar (West Tehran Customs), Aprin Dry Port or Mashhad Customs. A Ghabz-e Anbar warehouse receipt is provided. The buyer or their agent handles Iran import clearance, import duties and taxes, and customs pickup.

CPT and DAP allocate delivery and risk differently; the agreed Incoterms® rule and named place govern. Arrival at a terminal alone does not define risk transfer.

08

Questions before departure

Is the cargo never opened?

No. Customs inspection remains possible. The Wuqia direct configuration includes one Bukhara transload; fewer does not mean zero.

Does TIR guarantee faster transport?

No. We do not use TIR to promise faster delivery. The published ranges are operational references, measured from Wuqia.

Does TIR guarantee a lower freight rate?

No. Freight is quoted for the route and cargo. TIR is not presented as a price guarantee.

Where is cargo handed over?

At the agreed Iranian customs terminal: Shahriyar, Aprin or Mashhad. The buyer or their agent clears and collects the goods.

What is the Kyrgyzstan 0.4% item?

It is a separate item in the supplied Wuqia operating brief. Its classification, applicability and calculation basis must be confirmed in the shipment quotation; it is not a universal TIR tax claim.

What is needed for a quote?

Origin warehouse, destination customs terminal, HS code, gross weight and volume. Send these through WhatsApp or email.
