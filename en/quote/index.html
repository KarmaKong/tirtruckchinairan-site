Get a TIR truck quote with three details

  1. Origin warehouse: Yiwu / Shenzhen / Guangzhou / Shanghai.
  2. Destination customs: West Tehran Customs / Aprin dry port / Mashhad Customs.
  3. Cargo data: commodity, HS code, gross weight (kg), volume (CBM).

These three start the quote; add MSDS or loading dimensions where the cargo needs them.

Three things get left out: brand and model (customs needs them), whether the cargo carries batteries or liquids (which decides whether a curtain-side trailer can take it), and packing with stackability (which decides whether it fits one truck).

02

What happens after you send it

  1. We check commodity, HS code, gross weight, volume, origin warehouse and destination customs, and ask for MSDS or loading dimensions where they matter.
  2. We confirm the lane — direct, or transshipment via Wuqia — and the trailer that fits the 80 CBM and 22 t limit per truck.
  3. We email a formal quotation with a validity date and an RFQ number, so it can go through your purchasing process.

Two cases worth naming now: groupage starts at 1 CBM or 100 kg and is priced on actual volume and weight; a truck that exceeds 80 CBM or 22 t splits into two, quoted per truck rather than per cubic metre. Cargo needing MSDS — powders, liquids, chemical raw materials — stops at step one, so send it early.

A quotation states what it includes and what it does not; anything outside it — inland transfer beyond the destination terminal, for example — is listed separately rather than folded into the rate.
