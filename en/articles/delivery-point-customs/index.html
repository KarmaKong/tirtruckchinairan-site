Where a China–Iran TIR truck delivery ends

Two place names get mixed up in almost every enquiry: the crossing where the truck enters Iran, and the customs yard where the load is handed over. Only the second one is a delivery point.

21–23 dayscurrently, Wuqia to Tehran, direct truck, counted from the border.

The China hub to Wuqia leg is about 7 days on its own. It is never added to the border figure.

01

An entry crossing is not a handover point

On the Iranian side the truck comes in through Sarakhs or Lotfabad. Those two names answer one question only: which border the load enters through. They are not places where we hand cargo over, and they are not sold as destinations.

Two customs yards are what can be quoted as destinations: Mashhad Customs, and Tehran West Customs (Shahriyar / Aprin). If a supplier in Tehran asks where the truck stops, the answer is one of those two, not the border.

02

The handover happens inside the customs yard

Terms are CPT or DAP, at the destination customs terminal. Not door-to-door. Not DDP. Iranian import clearance is done by the buyer’s own licensed broker, and duties and import charges stay with the buyer.

“Arrived” means the trailer stands in the yard and the cargo is available for clearance. It does not include taking the goods off the trailer. Unloading, lashing and a crane lift are outside the freight quote.

Exit from China is Wuqia or Khorgos. Those are the two crossings we load through; Targhut and Alashankou are not running.

03

What the quote carries, and what it leaves out

Included: linehaul, China warehouse handling and export declaration, and the transit-agent fee abroad. Everything below sits outside it.

  • Destination clearance, duties, and any charge raised after the border.
  • Insurance, inspection, lashing, and a crane lift.
  • Any volume or weight above one truck.

The Kyrgyz 0.4% applies on the Wuqia line only, because that line crosses Kyrgyzstan. It is a tax, not freight, and the buyer pays it aside from the quote. The Khorgos line does not carry it.

04

Days and load limits are unchanged

Days are counted from the Wuqia crossing, currently Mashhad 18–21 by direct truck and 24–28 by transshipment, Tehran 21–23 and 26–30. These are operating figures, not a delivery promise, and they stop before Iranian clearance.

The Khorgos corridor has no published day count; it is confirmed per shipment. One trailer is a 13.6 m curtain-side, held to no more than 80 CBM and no more than 22 tonnes at the same time. Full truckload and groupage are both quoted, with groupage starting at 1 CBM or 100 kg.

05

Three details settle the handover point

  1. Origin hub: Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai.
  2. Destination customs: Mashhad, or Tehran West.
  3. Cargo: description, HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

Chemicals, dangerous goods and branded goods are reviewed on documents before loading is discussed. Lithium batteries need a full 16-section MSDS plus a UN packaging certificate. A review is not an acceptance.

06

Five questions to settle first

Is Sarakhs where my cargo is handed over?

No. Sarakhs is the entry crossing into Iran. Handover happens at Mashhad Customs or Tehran West Customs.

Can the truck unload at my warehouse?

No. Delivery is CPT or DAP at the destination customs yard. We do not run door-to-door, and we do not run DDP.

Who clears Iranian import customs?

The buyer’s licensed broker. We do not clear Iranian customs, and the duties are not in our quote.

Are unloading and a crane included?

No. Unloading, lashing and a crane lift sit outside the freight quote.

Is cargo insurance in the quote?

No. Insurance is arranged and paid by the buyer.
