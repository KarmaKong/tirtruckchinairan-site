TIR changes neither days nor freight

Buyers sometimes read TIR as a faster truck, or as a surcharge for paperwork. Neither reading holds. The seal changes how often the load is opened. It does not change the calendar, and it does not change the freight.

01

Two things actually change

Cargo is loaded at a China consolidation warehouse and sealed there, then moves under one carnet. Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Shanghai are the origin points we load from.

After that seal, unsealing and moving the cargo onto another truck happens less often. Less often is not never. A direct truck still changes once in Bukhara. A transshipment truck changes in Osh and again in Bukhara, and some loads change once more at the Iranian border.

03

Freight stays on the same footing

TIR does not change the freight. The same cargo is not repriced because a seal was applied or a carnet was issued. This page carries no freight figure. A formal quote goes out by email, with a validity date.

Included: linehaul, China warehouse handling and export clearance, and the transit-agent fee abroad. Not included: destination clearance, insurance, inspection, securing, crane lift, and charges that arise only after the border. The Kyrgyz 0.4% applies only on the Wuqia line, which crosses Kyrgyzstan. It is a tax, not freight, and the buyer pays it aside from the quote. The Khorgos line does not carry it.

04

Same curtain-side trailer

The truck is a tilt trailer, also called a curtain-side. It is not a container. Each truck is held to both limits at once: no more than 80 CBM, and no more than 22 tonnes. Whichever fills first is the one that stops the load.

Full truckload and groupage can both be quoted. Groupage starts from 1 CBM or 100 kg. Anything past one truck is checked separately. It is not forced into the same trailer.

05

Handover still stops at the customs yard

Sellable destinations are Mashhad Customs and Tehran West Customs (Shahriyar / Aprin). Sarakhs is the entry crossing. It is not a place we deliver to.

Terms are CPT or DAP only, at the destination customs yard. Not door-to-door. Not DDP. Iranian clearance is the buyer’s broker’s job. Exit is Wuqia or Khorgos only.

  1. Origin warehouse: Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai.
  2. Destination customs: Mashhad, or Tehran West.
  3. Cargo: description, HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

Chemicals, dangerous goods and branded goods are reviewed on documents before anyone talks about loading. Lithium batteries need a full 16-section MSDS plus a UN packaging certificate. A review is not an acceptance.

06

Five questions to settle first

Will TIR shorten the trip?

No. TIR does not change transit time.

Is freight higher because of TIR?

No. TIR does not change the freight. This page publishes no rate.

Is the trailer never opened?

No. Unsealing and truck changes are fewer, not zero.

Are there published days for Khorgos?

No. The Khorgos line is confirmed per shipment. No day count is published.

Can you deliver to the warehouse door?

No. Delivery is CPT or DAP at the destination customs yard. Not door-to-door, and not DDP.
