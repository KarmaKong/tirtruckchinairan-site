What a TIR truck is

A TIR truck is a way of running the freight, not a paperwork product. Your cargo is loaded and sealed at a consolidation warehouse in China — Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai — and moves under one carnet toward Iran via the Wuqia or Khorgos crossings.

What the seal reduces is the number of times cargo is unsealed and moved onto another truck. Fewer, not none.

TIR truck transit time and freight

A TIR truck does not change transit time and does not change freight — it is not a faster truck and not a pricier one. Days count from the Wuqia crossing: currently 18–21 days to Mashhad and 21–23 to Tehran on the direct service; 24–28 and 26–30 on the transshipment service.

Direct truck: one transload at Bukhara.

Transshipment service: transloads at Osh and Bukhara, with an additional transfer at the Iranian border for some shipments.

Sellable destinations and delivery terms

Sellable destinations are Mashhad Customs and Tehran West Customs (Shahriyar / Aprin). Sarakhs is the entry port, not a sellable destination.

Delivery is CPT / DAP at the destination customs yard — not door-to-door, not DDP. Iran clearance is handled by the buyer and their broker.

Truck type and load limits

Equipment is a 13.6 m tilt trailer; each truck is capped at 96 CBM and 25 t, both limits at once. Groupage starts from 1 CBM or 100 kg. Anything beyond one truck's capacity is priced separately.

Chemicals, dangerous goods and branded goods go through document review first; lithium batteries need a full 16-section MSDS and UN packaging. Review is not acceptance.
