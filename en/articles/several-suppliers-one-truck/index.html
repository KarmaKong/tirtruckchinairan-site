Several suppliers, one truck: sharing one TIR trailer

Two or three suppliers in different cities, one destination customs. Each cargo is received and counted separately at the China warehouse; the trailer is then sealed once for the whole load.

21–23 days from the Wuqia border to Tehran on the direct service.

For the domestic run from the China warehouse to Wuqia, allow about 7 days separately.

01

What “one truck, several shippers” means

It means two or more suppliers, each preparing cargo in its own city — Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai — with the same destination customs. None of them fills a trailer alone.

Cargo that meets the groupage threshold, from 1 CBM or 100 kg, can travel on the same trailer as someone else's. Once the combined load approaches one trailer's ceiling, one trailer is enough; whatever passes the ceiling needs a second one.

02

At the warehouse: each cargo counted on its own

Each supplier sends its cargo to a China consolidation warehouse. Every delivery is recorded separately: number of packages, dimensions, weight, with a label on each package showing the supplier, the shipment reference and the destination customs.

That separation is not paperwork; it is the difference between three pallets from three suppliers and one nameless heap. Without labels and a count, nobody at the destination customs yard can tell whose cargo is whose.

03

One seal for the whole trailer

When loading is finished the trailer is sealed at origin. There is one seal, not one per supplier, so every time it is opened, it is opened for the entire load.

On the road, unsealing and truck swaps are fewer, not zero: the direct service changes trucks once in Bukhara, the transshipment service in Osh and Bukhara. The structure is the same whether one supplier or three are on board.

04

Two ceilings: 96 CBM and 25 tonnes at once

The trailer is a 13.6 m curtain-side unit and each truck carries both limits at the same time: 96 CBM and 25 tonnes. Whichever fills first is the binding one — volume for light bulky cargo, weight for dense cargo.

The combined load of all suppliers is measured together, not cargo by cargo. If the total passes the ceiling, a second trailer is required and the excess is priced separately.

05

Timing: counted from the last cargo in

Each supplier ships on its own date, and the loading date is normally set by the last cargo to reach the warehouse. If part of the load needs to move earlier, it is reviewed case by case.

Transit time is counted from the Wuqia border: currently 18–21 days to Mashhad and 21–23 days to Tehran on the direct service; 24–28 and 26–30 days on the transshipment service. The domestic run from warehouse to Wuqia is about 7 days separately and is not added to these ranges. On the Khorgos line transit time is quoted per shipment.

06

Quoting several cargoes on one list

  1. Per cargo: commodity, HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM and package count.
  2. Shared by all: origin warehouse, destination customs and the approximate ready date of each cargo.
  3. The total: CBM and kg combined, so we know whether one trailer or two is needed.

Freight is to the destination customs yard under CPT / DAP; destination clearance, inspection, lashing and securing, crane lifting and insurance are not included. Iranian import clearance is handled by the buyer and its broker. No rate is published on this page; a quotation with validity comes by email or WhatsApp.

07

Questions about sharing one trailer

How many suppliers fit on one truck?

Usually two or three; the combined load must stay within 96 CBM and 25 tonnes.

Does each supplier get its own seal?

No. One seal covers the whole trailer.

What if one supplier is late?

The loading date normally moves back; shipping part of the load separately is reviewed case by case.

What is the minimum for groupage?

From 1 CBM or 100 kg.

Where can the cargo be delivered?

Mashhad customs or West Tehran customs (Shahriyar / Aprin); Sarakhs is the entry border, not a destination.
