The four guides

Where a China–Iran TIR truck delivery ends

Sarakhs and Lotfabad are entry crossings, not handover points. Delivery is CPT or DAP at Mashhad Customs or Tehran West Customs, and the buyer’s broker clears. Unloading and a crane sit outside the quote.

TIR changes neither days nor freight

A seal at the China warehouse plus one carnet. Unsealing is fewer, not zero: a direct truck still changes once in Bukhara. The day count stays on the current Wuqia table and the freight is not repriced.

Several suppliers, one trailer

Each supplier’s cargo is counted at the China hub, then one seal goes on the whole truck. Both limits apply to the total: 80 CBM and 22 tonnes per truck.

What a TIR truck is, and what it changes

It is a way of running the load, not a certificate on the wall: seal at origin, one carnet, fewer truck swaps, no change to transit time or freight.

Before any of this matters

The guides explain how the service works. They do not replace a quote, and they carry no freight figure. A formal quote goes out by email with a validity date, and it needs three things: origin hub (Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai), destination customs (Mashhad or Tehran West), and the cargo — description, HS code, gross weight in kg, volume in CBM.

Days behind every one of these pages are counted from the Wuqia crossing and are current operating ranges, not a promise. The Khorgos corridor is confirmed shipment by shipment, with no published day count. Exit is Wuqia or Khorgos only.

If your question is not covered here, ask it in the enquiry. Dimensions for machinery, an MSDS for chemicals: send those with the first message and the answer comes back faster.
