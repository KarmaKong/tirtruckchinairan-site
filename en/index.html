TIR truck transport in six steps: the Wuqia route

  1. Consolidate in China

    Deliver cargo to the Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai hub for packing-list and invoice checks and loading arrangements.

  2. Export through Wuqia

    Customs checks the cargo, applies the seal and endorses the TIR carnet for export release.

  3. Transit Kyrgyzstan

    Transshipment loads make their first transfer at Osh; direct trucks continue through.

  4. Transfer at Bukhara

    Direct loads make their single transfer here. Transshipment loads make their second.

  5. Cross Turkmenistan into Iran

    Enter through Sarakhs; some shipments make a further transfer at the Iranian border.

  6. Deliver to destination customs

    Cargo arrives at Mashhad Customs, Tehran West Customs or Aprin Dry Port. Delivery is to the customs terminal under CPT / DAP terms; the buyer arranges import clearance and pays duties.

Where can I consolidate cargo in China?

Yiwu, Shenzhen, Guangzhou or Shanghai. Specify your origin hub when requesting a quote.

Can I ship less than a full truckload?

Groupage starts at 1 CBM or 100 kg.

Does a direct truck involve a transfer?

On the Wuqia route, a direct load transfers once at Bukhara.

How do I get a transit time for Khorgos?

Send your origin hub, destination customs and cargo details. Transit time and transfer arrangements are confirmed case by case.

What cargo information is needed for a quote?

Description, HS code, gross weight and volume. Add dimensions for machinery and an MSDS for chemical materials.
